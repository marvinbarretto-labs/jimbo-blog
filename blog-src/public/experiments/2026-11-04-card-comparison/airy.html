What changed? What did not?

Only card padding, gaps and paragraph spacing differ between the two pages. The optional depth button adds a shadow. Content, ordering, typography, colour and available actions stay fixed.

This is not a user study: order is fixed, nobody is blinded, and choices do not carry between pages. It cannot tell us whether spacing, card content or shared discovery is better. It makes those questions easier to separate.
