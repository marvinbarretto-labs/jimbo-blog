The same deck, two distances

A comparison tool, not a survey. Three invented activity cards, with wording and order held fixed.

  • Compact: close enough to compare — less scrolling, less separation.
  • Airy: one thought at a time — more separation, more scrolling.

Each version also has a depth toggle. Add or remove shadows without changing spacing or your current choices. Choices live only in the open page and disappear on reload. Nothing is sent, saved or counted as feedback.

The question

When you prefer a deck, do you prefer the activities, the presentation, or the discovery with another person? This sketch separates just two presentation knobs. It does not implement shared swiping or test emotional response.

My design bet: compact is the stronger comparison surface; airy gives each proposal more ceremony. That is an opinion to try, not a result.
