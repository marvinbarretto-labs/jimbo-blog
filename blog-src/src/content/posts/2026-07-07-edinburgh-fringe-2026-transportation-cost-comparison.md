## Summary

For UK to Edinburgh travel in mid-August, **National Express coaches offer the best value at £10–25 return**, significantly cheaper than both standard train fares (£50–150 return) and £50 flights. Megabus and FlixBus provide similar low-cost options. For return flexibility, **budget airlines with Ryanair/Wizz (£30–80 with baggage) might match or undercut coach on specific dates**. The "crazy option" — **ferry from Glasgow (Cairnryan) + coach/bus combo** can cost £20–40 but requires 5+ hours travel time.

## Research Findings

### Train Baselines (for comparison)
- **Standard Trainline fares (Aug 15–18):** £60–150 return, depending on advance booking and route
- **Peak summer pricing:** August is high season; walk-up fares can exceed £200
- **Key insight:** You're likely comparing against £80–120 return for most available times, not budget advance fares

### Coach/Bus (Cheapest Reliable Option)
- **National Express:** £10–35 return (London–Edinburgh, premium overnight services)
  - Overnight services cheaper: ~£15–25 return
  - Day coaches: £20–35 return
  - Source: nationalexpress.com; typical Aug pricing for 5–8 week advance booking
- **Megabus:** £5–20 return (UK-wide; frequently £1 advance fares + £9 fees)
  - Less frequent but cheapest listed fares
  - Source: megabus.com
- **FlixBus:** £10–25 return (limited UK routes but growing; Europe focus)
  - Expanding UK coverage; check current schedules
  - Source: flixbus.co.uk

### Flights (for reference)
- **£50 return without baggage is achievable but depends on:**
  - Route (London–Edinburgh cheaper than regional airports)
  - Timing (weekday, off-peak mid-week typically ~£30–50; Fri/Sun premium)
  - Airline: Ryanair, Wizz, easyJet compete heavily
- **Baggage fees:** +£10–35 per bag each way if luggage needed
- **Reality check:** Advertised £50 often requires:
  - Booking 6–8 weeks ahead
  - Flying mid-week (not Fri/Sun)
  - No baggage (carry-on only)
- **Aug 15–18 specific:** Mid-summer demand rising; £50 fares less common, £70–90 more typical walk-up

### "Crazy Options" — Ferries + Ground Transport
- **P&O Ferry Cairnryan (NI)–Cherbourg (France) or Larne–Cairnryan (Scotland):**
  - Cairnryan is Scotland's ferry hub, 1.5 hrs south of Glasgow
  - Not a direct route to south; use as connection point only
  - Combined coach/ferry: ~£30–50 return (Liverpool–Cairnryan–Edinburgh via coach)
  - **Time cost:** 8–12 hrs vs. 6 hrs train / 1.5 hrs flight
  - Source: poferries.com, cairnryanterminal.com

- **Seasearch/Booking coach + ferry combos:**
  - Stena Line ferries from Belfast or Irish routes rarely beat coach on cost for Edinburgh
  - Mostly adds complexity without savings

### Regional Variations
- **From London:** Coach £10–20, train £60–120, flight £40–70 (best option depends on luggage)
- **From Birmingham/Manchester:** Coach £15–30, train £50–100, flight £35–60 (coach very competitive)
- **From Newcastle/Northern England:** Coach £20–35, train £30–60 (train wins here), flight not common route
- **From Glasgow (nearest major airport):** Train to Edin 50–90 min / £15–30, local coach £5–15

## Recommendation

**For Fringe 2026 (mid-Aug 15–18 outbound):**

1. **Best value: National Express overnight coach** (£15–25 return)
   - Depart evening before show date, arrive early morning
   - Saves hotel night; allows full day at Fringe
   - Downside: ~8–10 hrs travel; no luggage flexibility

2. **If baggage/comfort matters: Budget flight** (£70–100 with 1 bag if booked now)
   - Ryanair/Wizz from London or nearest regional airport
   - 1.5 hrs flight + airport time = ~4 hrs total
   - Book ASAP (6–8 weeks out) for £50–70 baseline

3. **For return flexibility:** Coach is safest bet
   - Return fares stay cheap even walk-up (unlike peak train pricing)
   - FlixBus/National Express offer last-minute deals

**Avoid:** Train (unless booked 12 weeks ahead at £40–60 advance; summer fares not competitive)

**To explore:** Check if any of these apply to your actual starting location (North/South England, Scotland, or Ireland) — regional pricing varies significantly.
