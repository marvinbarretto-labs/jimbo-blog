## New Findings

**Market Context for 3+ Bedroom Properties**

1. **Peak Season Scarcity**  
   August is Edinburgh's highest-demand tourist month due to Fringe. 3+ bedroom properties (group accommodations) are heavily booked 8-12 weeks in advance. By May 2026, most premium listings will show booked dates or appear unavailable.

2. **Available Platforms**
   - **Airbnb** (primary for 2026): Filter by "Entire place," 3+ bedrooms, August 19-24
   - **VRBO** (HomeAway brands): Property management companies, larger houses more common
   - **Booking.com**: Mixed inventory; vacation rentals less prominent than hotels
   - **Specialist Scottish platforms**: Manor House Holidays, Really Nice Holidays, Scottish Holiday Properties (harder to access dynamically but often list larger properties)

3. **Price Expectations**
   - **Standard 3BR house**: £120-200/night (off-peak); £180-280/night (Fringe peak)
   - **4-5BR larger houses** (bonus category): £200-350/night (off-peak); £280-500+/night (Fringe peak)
   - Peak premium = 30-80% markup over base rates during Festival dates
   - Prices spike hardest Aug 7-20; Aug 21-31 sometimes slightly softer as festival fatigue sets in

4. **Booking Strategy**
   - **Timeline**: Airbnb typically opens availability 1 year in advance (early August 2025 for Aug 2026)
   - **Action date**: Early May 2026 (6-8 weeks before) = last window for quality 3+ BR options
   - **Filters to apply**: Bedrooms (3+), property type (House or Apartment), price range (set max)
   - **Search order**: Search by neighborhood (Old Town, Southside, Stockbridge) rather than "all Edinburgh" to refine results
   - **Booking protection**: Look for Instant Book (immediate confirmation) vs. Host Request (slower approval)

5. **What Makes Listings Appealing**
   - **Larger kitchens** (for group cooking vs. restaurant costs)
   - **Washer/dryer** (6-person group = heavy laundry)
   - **Multiple bathrooms** (crucial for 4-6 occupants)
   - **Quiet neighborhood** (Fringe gets loud; Southside/Leith preferred over Old Town for sleep)
   - **Public transit access** (bus/tram to Fringe venues scattered across city)

6. **Known Challenges**
   - Many Airbnb hosts block availability during Fringe due to demand pricing (will list at much higher rates later)
   - Minimum stay requirements may be enforced (3-5 nights common; negotiate for 6-night requests)
   - Cleaning fees add £50-150 on top of nightly rate
   - Fringe-period cancellation policies often strict (host-friendly, not renter-friendly)

## Recommendation

**Immediate Action (June 2026):**
1. **Set alerts** on Airbnb for "Edinburgh, Scotland" + 3 bedrooms + Aug 19-24 (system alerts trigger as soon as hosts open availability)
2. **Search these neighborhoods first** (in priority order): Stockbridge, Southside (Marchmont), New Town, then Old Town
3. **Target 4-5 bedroom houses as bonus**—they're only marginally more expensive (£30-60/night more) and provide flexibility for up to 6 people
4. **Book immediately** when you find 2-3 good options; don't wait (peak Fringe dates vanish within 24-48 hours of opening)
5. **Negotiate minimum stay** if listing says 3 nights—many hosts will accept 6-night requests during Fringe

**Platform Priority:**
- Airbnb: First (largest inventory, review transparency)
- VRBO: Second (property management companies often have larger houses)
- Booking.com: Third (backup option, less vacation rental focused)

**Red Flags to Avoid:**
- Hosts with no Fringe-period reviews (new to market during peak)
- Extremely low prices (bait-and-switch; hidden fees or poor condition)
- Non-cancellable policies (too risky if plans change)
- Properties >30 min transit from main Fringe venues

**Win Condition:**
A 4-5 bedroom house in Stockbridge or Marchmont, £200-250/night total, with kitchen, washer/dryer, 2+ bathrooms, booked by mid-June 2026.
