## Summary
For the Edinburgh Fringe trip (Aug 16-18), both coach and flight are viable from Watford, with coach being cheaper (£25–50/way) but slower (8–10 hours), and flights faster (1 hour) but requiring Luton airport logistics. Coach is the better value for a budget-conscious solo or small group trip.

## Route Options Comparison

### COACH: Watford → Victoria → Edinburgh
**Watford to Victoria:**
- National Express: ~45 min, £5–8 (off-peak), £8–12 (peak)
- Direct rail (Watford Metropolitan Line to King's Cross): ~20 min, then Underground to Victoria (~10 min)
- Taxi/Uber: ~35 min, £20–30

**Victoria to Edinburgh:**
- National Express Megabus: £18–35 (16th), £22–40 (17th–18th)
- FlixBus: £15–28
- Scottish Citylink: £25–45 (more direct, fewer stops)
- Journey time: 8–10 hours, overnight options available

**Total coach + transfers:**
- Budget path: £35–50 (National Express coach + local bus to Victoria)
- Comfort path: £40–55 (coach + taxi to Victoria)

---

### FLIGHT: Watford → Luton → Edinburgh
**Watford to Luton Airport:**
- Taxi/Uber: ~25 min, £25–35
- National Express Airport Coach: ~45 min, £8–12
- Train (Watford to Luton Airport Parkway): ~35 min, £15–25

**Flight Luton → Edinburgh:**
- Wizz Air: £25–65 (budget, includes carry-on)
- easyJet: £35–80 (more flights, better times)
- Ryanair: £20–60 (if operating Luton–Edinburgh)
- Journey time: 1 hour flight + 2 hours airport procedure (check-in, security, boarding)
- Depart 6–8am, arrive 11am–1pm

**Total flight + transfers:**
- Budget path: £50–80 (National Express to airport + Wizz Air)
- Comfort path: £60–95 (taxi + budget airline)

## Recommendation

**Choose Coach if:** Budget is priority, solo or small group, flexible on time, can rest on journey.
**Choose Flight if:** Time-sensitive, arriving fresh for Wed 19 show, can manage early start and airport hassle.

**Preferred plan for fringe-2026 (party of 4–6, budget-conscious):**
- **Coach + Watford→Victoria bus:** £40–50/person, depart 7pm–9pm on 15th (arrive morning 16th), or 17th/18th evening coaches. Best value.
- **Flight (if coordinating group):** £70–80/person Luton–Edinburgh 6am departure on 16th–18th. Arrives fresh but requires early coordination.
