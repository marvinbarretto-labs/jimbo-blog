## Summary

This document provides a methodology and reusable framework for Marvin to identify, score, and prioritize 15-20 warm contacts from his existing network who are non-technical decision-makers likely to benefit from AI product translation advisory. The framework uses relationship warmth and AI translator fit as scoring axes, with templated output for tracking outreach readiness.

## Context

Marvin's AI translator positioning targets non-technical decision-makers (MDs, founders, CEOs, CTOs who can't code) who need help understanding and implementing AI solutions. Success depends on converting existing warm relationships into first anchor clients via structured outreach, not cold prospecting.

## Network Sources & Identification Strategy

**Where to find candidates:**

1. **Previous clients/employers** — People who've hired you, worked with you at Fourfold, LocalShout, Jimbo, or other projects
2. **Collaborators** — Other founders, partners, co-speakers at events, workshop attendees
3. **Referral network** — People who know people (ask existing contacts for warm introductions)
4. **Community ties** — LocalShout community members, Tailscale community, maker/founder groups
5. **Consulting history** — Anyone you've advised informally

**Candidate criteria — must satisfy ALL of:**
- Non-technical or non-coding (CTO/MD/Founder, not VP Engineering)
- In a decision-making role (can spend budget, approve pivots)
- Known to you (warm contact, not cold)
- Active in a role that might need AI product translation (SaaS, fintech, healthtech, logistics, etc.)

## Scoring Framework

### Relationship Warmth (3-point scale)

| Warmth | Definition | Signal |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| **Warm** | Recent contact (within 6 months), direct relationship | Text/call/coffee in last 6mo; explicitly asked for advice; personal connection |
| **Medium** | Known but not recent (6-18 months), some relationship depth | Worked together, LinkedIn connection, mutual friend vouches |
| **Cold** | Indirect connection or stale (18+ months), minimal depth | "Met at event", LinkedIn only, mutual friend but no direct contact |

### AI Translator Fit (3-point scale)

| Fit | Definition | Signal |
|-----|-----------|--------|
| **High** | Currently building AI product or evaluating AI vendors | Recently mentioned AI challenge, hiring ML engineers, published on AI topic |
| **Medium** | Company/role where AI is emerging need but not immediate | SaaS/fintech/logistics player, executive role, likely to face AI decision in 12mo |
| **Low** | Could benefit from advisor but AI is not near-term need | Traditional business, early-stage, AI not currently top 3 priorities |

### Combined Scoring

**Priority = Warmth + AI Fit**

| Warmth | High Fit | Medium Fit | Low Fit |
|--------|----------|-----------|---------|
| Warm | **P1** (6 pts) | **P2** (5 pts) | P3 (4 pts) |
| Medium | **P2** (5 pts) | P3 (4 pts) | P4 (3 pts) |
| Cold | P3 (4 pts) | P4 (3 pts) | P5 (2 pts) |

**Target distribution for 15-20 contacts:**
- Aim for 3-5 P1 candidates (top outreach wave)
- 5-7 P2 candidates (second wave)
- 5-7 P3+ candidates (pipeline, lower conversion expected)

## Identification Workflow (Step-by-step)

1. **Brain dump** — Spend 30 min listing everyone you know in decision-making roles (no filtering yet)
2. **Filter for criteria** — Remove anyone who's purely technical, never directly contacted you, or in roles that don't make spend decisions
3. **Score warmth** — Mark each as warm/medium/cold based on contact recency
4. **Score AI fit** — Research each person's current company/role news; mark fit level
5. **Assign priority** — Use combined score to rank into outreach waves
6. **Top 5 selection** — Choose your P1 candidates for first wave (aim for 3-5, may be fewer)
7. **Validate** — For each P1, write a one-line rationale; if you can't articulate it in one line, reconsider

## Why This Matters

Warm relationships are your unfair advantage. Cold outreach to "AI translator" as a title has low credibility. Warm referrals from people who've worked with you convert at 5-10x higher rates because:
- They've seen your ability to explain complex things simply
- They trust your judgment (you've already advised them)
- Social proof works — a CEO friend saying "Marvin helped us navigate AI" > LinkedIn DM
