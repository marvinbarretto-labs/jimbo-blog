## Summary

UK AI advisory day rates cluster into three distinct tiers: entry anchor at £500–750/day (independent, early-career specialists), mid at £800–1,000/day (experienced freelancers and boutique firms), and ceiling at £1,200–2,500/day (London-based senior specialists, fractional CTOs, and LLM expertise). All tiers support equivalent weekly rates (5-day weeks).

## Market Context

The UK AI advisory market in 2026 shows strong demand for AI expertise, particularly in LLM integration and responsible AI governance, with supply-side scarcity pushing specialist rates 15–50% above baseline. London commands a 20–40% premium versus regional rates; IR35 contractor status and specialization (fintech, healthcare, AI/ML) add further variability. Most independent advisors offer flexible pricing: day rates for short-term advisory, retainers for ongoing support, and fixed project fees for defined outcomes.

## Three-Tier Rate Card

### Tier 1: Entry Anchor (Minimum Acceptable)
**Day Rate:** £500–750  
**Weekly Equivalent:** £2,500–3,750 (5 days)

**Who this tier represents:**
- Early-career AI consultants with 2–4 years' experience
- Generalist AI/ML advisors without specialization
- Freelancers building portfolio and client base
- Regional (non-London) independent practitioners

**Rationale:**
This floor price establishes a market-credible minimum for professional advisory work. Charging below this signals either inexperience or desperation; above it requires demonstrated value. This tier is competitive for project-based work, readiness audits (£3,500–15,000), and short-term advisory engagements where depth of specialization is less critical than broad AI/ML knowledge.

**Sources:** UK market baseline from FutureFill.AI (median £550/day), NRB Consulting, and Contractordayrate.co.uk benchmarks.

---

### Tier 2: Mid (Realistic Starting Ask)
**Day Rate:** £800–1,000  
**Weekly Equivalent:** £4,000–5,000 (5 days)

**Who this tier represents:**
- Experienced freelance AI consultants (4–8 years)
- Mid-size boutique AI/ML consulting firms
- Specialists with proven track records (case studies, testimonials)
- Regional roles in high-cost areas (Manchester, Edinburgh)
- Senior AI engineers working fractionally or between contracts

**Rationale:**
This is where the market median sits for independent consultants and mid-tier service providers. Sufficient to attract experienced practitioners; realistic for clients expecting professional delivery and measurable outcomes. This tier is appropriate for multi-week engagements, strategy sprints (£12,000–40,000), adoption programmes, and advisory retainers (£3,000–8,000/month for part-time support). Clients at this price expect delivery, not just ideas.

**Sources:** NRB Consulting (£700–900 average), Whitehat (£600–900 boutique), and market medians from AI Consulting Costs UK 2026 reports.

---

### Tier 3: Ceiling (Aspirational with Proof)
**Day Rate:** £1,200–2,500+  
**Weekly Equivalent:** £6,000–12,500+ (5 days)

**Who this tier represents:**
- Senior AI/ML architects and fractional CTOs (8+ years, proven leadership)
- LLM integration and GenAI specialists (15–50% premium over baseline)
- Responsible AI and governance experts (25–40% premium)
- London-based or internationally recognized practitioners
- Specialists with IP, published research, or rare expertise (fintech AI, healthcare ML compliance)

**Rationale:**
Premium pricing is earned through documented expertise, case studies, and client outcomes. At the ceiling, advisors take on strategic accountability: they're shaping architecture, advising on vendor selection, or leading high-stakes adoption. This tier reflects actual demand in the market—fractional CTOs in London command £1,200–1,600/day regionally and £1,500–2,000/day in London; specialists in scarce domains (GenAI, compliance) exceed £2,000/day. This is the rate Marvin should target once track record is established, given the scarcity of demonstrated AI expertise in the market.

**Sources:** Fractional CTO UK benchmarks (Elite Fractional CxO, CodPal), Whitehat (LLM premium +30–50%), and specialist surveys showing AI/ML as top earner in 2026.

## Recommended Positioning

**Starting point:** Tier 2 (£800–1,000/day) for first client engagements, emphasizing track record and outcomes.  
**Evolution:** Move to Tier 3 (£1,200–1,600/day London) once 3–5 case studies are in hand and you can demonstrate measurable client impact (adoption adoption, efficiency gains, revenue uplift).  

**Pricing model flexibility:**
- **Day rates** for short advisory (1–5 days): Use tier pricing directly
- **Weekly retainers** (part-time): £4,000–8,000/month for Tier 2 equivalent; £8,000–15,000+ for Tier 3
- **Project-based** (fixed scope): Use day rate × estimated days + 20% for scope risk

**Negotiation tactic:** Quote Tier 2 as entry; if client pushes back on price, offer reduced scope (fewer days, narrower focus) rather than discounting rate. Discounting trains clients to expect discounts; scope reduction maintains rate integrity.
