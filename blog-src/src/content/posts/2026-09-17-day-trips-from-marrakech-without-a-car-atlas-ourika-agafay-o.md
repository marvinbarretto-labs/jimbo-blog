## Summary

From Riad Sabah, you have six viable day trip options ranging from 30km (Agafay) to 180km (Aït Benhaddou). Best options for your trip: **Ourika Valley** for a 1-day accessible valley hike with Berber villages (60-90 min each way, €17/person shared), or **Ouzoud Falls** for a dramatic waterfall escape (2.5-3 hrs, €19/person). For a splurge or second day, **Aït Benhaddou** (UNESCO kasbah, 3-4 hrs each way) justifies a full 10-12 hour day.

## Each destination in detail

### 1. Ourika Valley & Setti Fatma ⭐ Best for first day

**What you do:**
Valley hike to Setti Fatma's waterfalls, visit to Berber villages and argan cooperative, lunch at riverside tagine restaurant, swimming (seasonal).

**Travel:** 
- Depart Riad Sabah: 30 min to medina edge, 1–1.5 hrs by road to Setti Fatma
- Return: 1.5–2 hrs to Marrakech
- Total day: 8–9 hrs

**Cost (for 2 people):**
- Shared tour: €17/pp (£27 total)
- Private AC car + driver: €120 (~£96)
- DIY: ~50–60 MAD total (~£4–5) for grand taxis each way

**Why it works:**
Easy logistics, stunning valley scenery, genuine Berber interaction, no serious hiking required. The road itself is picturesque with stops at argan cooperatives and viewpoints.

**Recommendation:** Book a shared tour (GetYourGuide, Viator) or use grand taxis from Bab Doukala. Morning departure 08:00–09:00 gets you there by 10:00, lunch by 13:00, back by 18:00. Minimal hassle.

---

### 2. Imlil & High Atlas foothills

**What you do:**
1–2 hr hike through Toubkal National Park to hidden waterfalls, Berber village visits, 3-course mountain lunch, views of Mount Toubkal (4,167m).

**Travel:**
- ~70 km, 1.5–2 hrs by car
- Return: 1.5–2 hrs
- Total: 7–8 hrs including 1–2 hr hike

**Cost (for 2 people):**
- Shared tour: €50/pp (~£40 total); some as low as $30 USD (~£24)
- Private: €50–100/pp
- DIY: ~60–80 MAD (~£5–6.50) transport, organize hike guide locally (~50 MAD)

**Why it works:**
Proper mountain scenery, easy-to-moderate hike, Berber lunch included, North Africa's highest peak visible. Quieter than Ourika.

**Recommendation:** Suitable if you want "real" hiking but not strenuous (no climbing, mostly village paths and gentle mountain trails). Book shared tour for best value. Less touristy than Ourika but more strenuous.

---

### 3. Agafay Desert ⭐ Best for energy, late afternoon start

**What you do:**
2-hr quad bike ride over rocky dunes, 1-hr camel ride, sunset, traditional dinner with live music and fire show, optional swimming pool.

**Travel:**
- Only 30 km, 45 min–1 hr from medina
- Half-day (4–6 hrs) or full evening (6–7 hrs with dinner show)

**Cost (for 2 people):**
- Full package (quad + camel + dinner + show): €95/pp (~£152 total)
- Half-day (quad + camel only): ~€40–50/pp (~£64–80)
- DIY: ~40 MAD transport (~£3) + activity costs (expensive without booking package)

**Why it works:**
Closest destination, fastest return, different landscape (pseudo-desert steppe, not Sahara). Great for afternoon energy, evening activity. Dinner show is tourist-focused but fun.

**Recommendation:** Skip the quad bikes if you prefer; camel ride alone is memorable and less jarring. Book 15:00–16:00 start, back by 22:00. Works well on a hot day (cooler evening). Package deals on GetYourGuide/Viator are efficient.

---

### 4. Ouzoud Falls ⭐ Most impressive waterfall

**What you do:**
Hike around Morocco's highest waterfall (110m cascade), boat ride at the base, Berber village visits, swimming holes, lunch.

**Travel:**
- ~150 km, 2.5–3 hrs by road
- Return: 2.5–3 hrs
- Total: 10–11 hrs (tight for a day trip)

**Cost (for 2 people):**
- Shared tour: €19/pp (~£30 total), daily 08:30 departures
- Private: €35–120/pp
- DIY: 50 MAD bus (~£4) + 40 MAD taxi (~£3) from Azilal + ~20 MAD boat ride (~£1.60)
- Total DIY: ~110 MAD (~£8.80)

**Why it works:**
Spectacular waterfall, genuinely photogenic, feels wilder than Ourika, lunch at poolside cafés (no entrance fee). 

**Caution:**
Day trip is tight — you'll leave by 08:30, arrive 11:00–11:30, hike/boat 2–3 hrs, lunch 1–1.5 hrs, depart by 16:00 to be back by 19:00–20:00. Less time to lounge than Ourika.

**Recommendation:** Shared tour is best value and takes logistics off you. Doable if you're energetic and don't mind a long travel day. DIY is cheaper but requires coordination in French/Arabic with local taxis.

---

### 5. Essaouira (Atlantic coast)

**What you do:**
Coastal town, fishing harbor, fresh seafood lunch, medina wandering, beach time, artisan markets. Windy but atmospheric.

**Travel:**
- ~160 km, 2.5–3 hrs by bus or car
- CTM/Supratours buses: 7 daily (07:45, 09:00, 10:45, 12:00, 14:45, 16:45, 19:00)
- Return: 2.5–3 hrs
- Total: 8–9 hrs if you leave early and return evening

**Cost (for 2 people):**
- Bus (CTM/Supratours): 80–100 MAD/pp (~£6.40–8 per person, £13–16 total)
- Guided day trip: varies (€40–100/pp) but unnecessary
- Seafood lunch: 100–150 MAD/pp (~£8–12 per person)

**Why it works:**
Most relaxed day trip, sea breeze, excellent seafood, bohemian vibe, no hiking. Feels different from mountain destinations.

**Caution:**
Very windy (Atlantic trade winds), can be chilly even in September. Summer-beach energy is low; it's more artsy than sunbathing.

**Recommendation:** DIY by bus is simplest and cheapest. Leave 09:00 bus, arrive 12:00, lunch 13:00–14:30, beach/explore 14:30–17:00, return 17:00 bus, back 20:00. Skip for pure beach relaxation; ideal if you want coastal change of pace. Book return bus ticket at arrival for peace of mind.

### 6. Aït Benhaddou (UNESCO kasbah) — Splurge day

**What you do:**
Walk through UNESCO-listed mudbrick kasbah (used in *Game of Thrones*, *Lawrence of Arabia*), visit Atlas Film Studios, high-altitude Tizi n Tichka Pass (2,260m), panoramic landscapes.

**Travel:**
- 180 km, 3–4 hrs each way via Tizi n Tichka Pass
- Return: 3–4 hrs
- Total: 10–12 hrs including kasbah + studio visits

**Cost (for 2 people):**
- Group tour: €23/pp (~£37 total) for 14–17-person groups
- Small group (6–8 ppl): €50–80/pp (~£80–128)
- Private: €100+/pp
- Lunch (not included): ~140 MAD/pp (~£11 per person, £22 total)
- Film studio guide: +€3/pp (~£5 total)

**Why it works:**
Most dramatic landscape, UNESCO site, incredible photo opportunity, deepest cultural experience. 10–12 hrs is long but justified by scenery.

**Caution:**
Long day. Return time is 19:00–20:00 at earliest. Altitude (2,260m pass) can feel thin at fast pace. Lunch typically extra and overpriced.

**Recommendation:** Book a small group or private tour (GetYourGuide, Viator, Moroccan Journeys) with pickup by 08:00, include breakfast. This is your "big" day trip if you have it. Skip the film studio if time is tight (it's touristy filler). Prefer a 2-night trip to Ouarzazate + Aït Benhaddou if you can reschedule, but as a 1-day push, it's doable.

## My recommendation for your trip (Thu 17 – Wed 23 Sep)

**Best pairing for 2 spare days (you have 6 nights, so 5 full days in Marrakech, minus city days):**

**Day 1 (suggest Friday 18 Sep):** Ourika Valley shared tour
- Easiest, most accessible, great for acclimatization
- 8–9 hrs total, early start (08:00), back by 18:00, evening free

**Day 2 (suggest Monday 21 or Tuesday 22 Sep):** Essaouira by bus or Agafay at sunset
- If you want coast and calm: Essaouira (DIY bus, £13 total)
- If you want adventure and stay local: Agafay quad + camel + dinner (€95/pp, back by 22:00)

**Skip:** Aït Benhaddou for this trip (save for a future 2-night trip), or do it **only** if you have a spare day and rent a car. Ouzoud is brilliant but the 2.5–3 hr drive makes it tight for one of your 5 full days.

**If you hire a car for one day (your note mentions this is open):**
Use it for Aït Benhaddou + Ouarzazate (10–12 hrs, one day). Then do Ourika + Agafay/Essaouira by tours/bus.

## Logistics summary

**Booking shared tours:** GetYourGuide, Viator, or Marrakech-desert-trips.com all offer daily departures. Book 1 day in advance; most have 24-hr free cancellation. Pickup from medina is typically 08:00–09:00.

**Public transport (Essaouira):** CTM or Supratours buses depart Bab Doukala station. Buy return ticket on arrival. Buses are comfortable, A/C, mostly on time.

**Grand taxis (Ourika, local hikes):** Bab Doukala or Bab Agnaou stations. Cheaper than tours, less hand-holding. Negotiate return pickup time with driver.

**Private car hire:** If you rent a car (e.g., Hertz, Budget at airport), expect €30–50/day + fuel (~10–15 MAD/liter for petrol). Useful only for Aït Benhaddou or a 2-destination day.

**Weather:** Late September, temperatures drop to 28–32°C by day, 15–18°C at night. Agafay can feel cold at sunset. Ourika valley is cooler (~24°C). Essaouira is windy. Pack layers.
