## Summary

Marrakech offers five distinct evening experiences for couples: rooftop bars (cocktails 80–180 MAD/€8–18) with sunset views, authentic street food at Jemaa el-Fnaa (40–60 MAD per person), dinner-shows with live Gnawa music (600–900 MAD per person), traditional or spa hammams (100–600 MAD per person), and neighbourhood bars split between laid-back Gueliz (local vibe, 45–150 MAD drinks) and energetic Hivernage clubs (200–300 MAD entry, 1 AM–5 AM). Budget roughly 800–1,500 MAD per person per evening across all activities combined. September offers ideal timing—shoulder season with warm evenings, fewer crowds, lower prices than peak summer, and manageable sunset timing (7–7:30 PM).

The prior context confirms September as a **shoulder season** ideal for travel: warm (20–24°C) but not overwhelming heat, no Friday prayer closures in evening venues (only morning sights), and fewer aggressive touts.

### 2. Jemaa el-Fnaa Street Food at Night (8–10 PM Peak)

**Safety & Quality Strategy**: The square is heavily policed and food from busy stalls with local Moroccan families is safe. Avoid aggressive touts; walk the entire square before choosing a stall.

**What to Order** (and where):
- **Harira** (chickpea-lentil soup) — warming, authentic
- **Lamb Tangia** (slow-cooked stew) — specialty
- **Merguez Sausages** (grilled spiced sausage)
- **Mechoui** (slow-roasted lamb) — seek dedicated "Mechoui Alley" near square, 11 AM–2 PM peak, but available evenings

**Recommended Strategy**: Stall 44 ("where locals eat") serves authentic dishes without tourist markup. Avoid Stall 35 (known for inflated bills and bait-and-switch).

**Pricing**:
- Light snacks: 10–30 MAD
- Full meal: 40–80 MAD
- **Complete evening meal per person: 40–60 MAD** (cheapest authentic dinner option)

**Essential Tips**:
- Confirm prices **before** sitting
- Watch for inflated final bills; bring small bills for exact payment
- Hydrate well; food is salty and filling
- Best time: 8–10 PM (peak food variety, vibrant energy)

### 4. Hammams — Traditional & Spa Options

**Pricing & Experience Tiers:**

| Type | Price Range | What's Included | Duration | Best For |
|------|------------|-----------------|----------|----------|
| Public Hammam (local) | 40–80 MAD | Entry only; scrubber extra (+50 MAD) | 30–45 min | Budget, authentic, segregated |
| Boutique Spa | 150–400 MAD | Entry + scrub; better facilities | 60–90 min | Couples, cleaner, mid-range |
| Luxury Riad/Spa | 500–1,500 MAD | Full packages, massage, premium | 90–120 min | Ultimate experience, comfort |

**Top Specific Hammams:**

- **Heritage Spa** (near Ben Youssef Madrasa): 200–400 MAD for scrub-and-massage combo. Central medina location.
- **Hammam Dar el-Bacha** (medina): Authentic 16th-century bathhouse with star-shaped skylights. Public hammam vibe (~80 MAD). Local clientele.
- **Hammam Mouassine** (medina): Highly respected, strict segregation, excellent scrubbers (~80 MAD entry + 50 MAD scrubber).
- **Les Bains de Marrakech** (Hivernage): 300–700 MAD range, boutique quality.

**What to Bring**:
- Towel (own or rent)
- Flip-flops/shower shoes
- Savon beldi (black soap) — or buy locally (~5 MAD)
- Kessa glove (exfoliating mitt)
- Swimsuit (optional, private facilities only)

**Etiquette**:
- Gender segregation: Strict in public hammams; women topless is normal; men keep underwear on
- Tip scrubber: 20–50 MAD
- No photography inside
- Hydrate before and after
- Avoid Thursday evenings and Friday mornings (peak local use)
- Best: Weekday afternoons (2–5 PM), quieter and more authentic

**Recommendation for Couples**: Choose a **boutique riad hammam** (300–500 MAD) rather than public hammam on first visit. Provides cultural immersion with comfort, clear service, and cleaner facilities.

## Recommended 6-Evening Itinerary

**Goal**: Mix sunset rooftops, authentic food, live music, cultural experiences, and nightlife. Budget flexibility to explore both casual and premium options.

### Evening 1: Medina Rooftop → Jemaa el-Fnaa Street Food

- **6:30 PM**: Sunset at **Le Salama** (rooftop bar, Atlas views, cocktail 80–180 MAD)
- **8:30 PM**: Walk to **Jemaa el-Fnaa**, eat at Stall 44 or similar (40–60 MAD per person)
- **Activity**: Street performers, live music, energy
- **Cost per person**: ~200 MAD (~€19)

### Evening 2: Hammam + Gueliz Dinner & Nightlife

- **4–5 PM**: **Heritage Spa or Hammam Dar el-Bacha** (300–400 MAD boutique, or 80–100 MAD public)
- **7:30 PM**: Dinner at **Grand Café de la Poste** (150–250 MAD, colonial charm)
- **9:30 PM**: Cocktails at **Baromètre** (80–150 MAD)
- **Cost per person**: ~650–800 MAD if boutique hammam; ~450–550 MAD if public

### Evening 3: Dinner-Show with Live Gnawa Music (Hivernage)

- **8:30 PM**: **Comptoir Darna** dinner-show (600–900 MAD per person, includes dinner + Gnawa musicians, belly dancers, acrobats until 10:30 PM)
- **11:00 PM**: Transition to club dancing if desired (no additional entry)
- **Cost per person**: 600–900 MAD (~€56–84)

### Evening 4: Gueliz Rooftop Happy Hour + Late Dinner

- **6:00 PM**: **Kechmara** happy hour (cocktails 50% off, 50–100 MAD, 18:00–19:30 window)
- **7:30 PM**: Dinner at **Liva** (wood-fired pizzas, 130–250 MAD, kitchen until 00:30)
- **9:30 PM**: Option: Jazz at **Montecristo** (live rock/blues, upscale vibe) OR bars at The Mix
- **Cost per person**: ~350–450 MAD

### Evening 5: Street Food + Casual Music Experience

- **7:00 PM**: Pre-dinner cocktail at **The Mix** rooftop (Gueliz, 170–290 MAD)
- **8:30 PM**: **Jemaa el-Fnaa** street food again, or try **Café Clock** for free Gnawa music (~7:30 PM)
- **Nightlife**: Bar-hopping Gueliz (80–150 MAD cocktails) OR Hivernage club if feeling energetic
- **Cost per person**: ~350–550 MAD depending on club choice

### Evening 6: Luxury Spa Experience or Repeat Favorite

**Option A (Luxury):**
- **6:00 PM**: Luxury riad or resort spa hammam with massage (500–700 MAD)
- **8:00 PM**: Fine dinner in medina (Nomad or similar, 250–500 MAD)
- **10:00 PM**: Medina rooftop nightcap (**El Fenn**, **Maison MK**, drinks 100–200 MAD)
- **Cost per person**: ~850–1,000 MAD

**Option B (Repeat Favorite + Budget):**
- Repeat Evening 1 or 4; explore a rooftop or venue not yet visited
- **Cost per person**: ~200–450 MAD

## Practical Notes for Your Trip (Sep 17–23, 2026)

1. **Booking**: Reserve rooftop bars and dinner-shows 3+ days in advance. Hammams and Gueliz restaurants generally walk-in friendly.

2. **Alcohol Availability**: Licensed rooftop bars, Gueliz venues, and Hivernage clubs serve full alcohol. Tea-only cafés do not. Medina can feel conservative; alcohol is legal but less visible.

3. **Transportation**:
   - Medina rooftops: Walkable from Riad Sabah
   - Gueliz: 15–20 minute walk from medina, or taxi (20–30 MAD)
   - Hivernage: Taxi-required from medina (25–40 MAD)

4. **Timing**:
   - Sunset: 7–7:30 PM (perfect rooftop entry time)
   - Street food peak: 8–10 PM
   - Shows: 9:30–11:30 PM typically
   - Clubs: 1 AM–5 AM peak energy

5. **Dress Code Summary**:
   - Rooftops (medina/Gueliz): Smart casual; collared shirt for upscale
   - Hivernage clubs: Strictly enforced—closed shoes, no athletic wear
   - Street food: Casual/comfort appropriate

6. **Tipping**:
   - Hammam scrubber: 20–50 MAD
   - Restaurant/bar staff: 10% standard; round up
   - Street performers (Jemaa): Appreciated, 10–20 MAD

## Recommendation

**For a couple seeking authentic immersion**: Alternate between medina rooftops (traditional atmosphere, souk/mountain views), street food at Jemaa el-Fnaa (cheapest, most authentic dinner), and one dinner-show for live Gnawa music (cultural centerpiece). Skip Hivernage clubs on first trip unless you're night-dancing enthusiasts; Gueliz bars offer better value and local atmosphere.

**Best value evening**: Jemaa el-Fnaa street food (50 MAD) + rooftop bar (120 MAD) + walking exploration = 170 MAD with maximum atmosphere.

**Worth splurging on**: Comptoir Darna dinner-show (800 MAD for two) as the cultural highlight; Heritage Spa hammam (350 MAD) for comfort on a rainy day; one fine dinner at Nomad or Grand Café de la Poste (250–350 MAD) for elegance.
