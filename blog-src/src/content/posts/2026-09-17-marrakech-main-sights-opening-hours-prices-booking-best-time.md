## Summary

Marrakech's eight main sights operate 9 AM–7 PM (most), with ticket prices ranging 40–100 MAD. Three sites require advance booking: YSL Museum (mandatory online-only, 100 MAD), Jardin Majorelle (100 MAD, recommended online), and optional for others. To beat heat and crowds during your Thu 17–Wed 23 Sep visit: start mornings 8–10 AM at eastern medina palaces (Bahia, Ben Youssef), shift to gardens early afternoon (Majorelle, Le Jardin Secret), leave souks and tanneries for late afternoon (4–6 PM). Friday midday prayer (noon–3 PM) closes small shops but not museums; major sites run normal hours.

---

## Detailed Site Information

### 1. **Bahia Palace**
- **Hours:** 9:00 AM–5:00 PM daily
- **Ticket:** 100 MAD (~€9)
- **Advance booking:** Not required; walk-in available
- **Time needed:** 1–1.5 hours
- **Crowds:** Peak midday; quieter morning (9–10 AM) and late afternoon (4–5 PM)
- **Best time:** Early morning or after 4 PM
- **Notes:** Central medina location; accessible on foot from Riad Sabah

### 2. **Ben Youssef Madrasa**
- **Hours:** 9:00 AM–7:00 PM daily
- **Ticket:** 50 MAD (foreign adults); 20 MAD (Moroccan adults); 10 MAD (children under 12)
- **Group rate:** 30 MAD per person (21+ people)
- **Advance booking:** Online ticketing available but not required
- **Time needed:** 1–1.5 hours
- **Crowds:** Peak 11 AM–3 PM; quieter early morning and after 5 PM
- **Best time:** Before 9:30 AM or after 5 PM
- **Notes:** Adjacent to souks; often combined with morning palace visits

### 3. **Jardin Majorelle**
- **Hours:** 9:00 AM–6:00 PM daily (last entry varies by season)
- **Ticket:** 100 MAD; online booking recommended
- **Advance booking:** Yes, recommended; online at tickets.jardinmajorelle.com
- **Time needed:** 1.5–2 hours
- **Crowds:** Peak 10 AM–4 PM; quieter early morning (before 9:30 AM) and late afternoon (after 5 PM)
- **Best time:** Early morning (8–9:30 AM) or after 5 PM
- **Notes:** Out of medina; quintessential blue garden; perfect for mid-afternoon heat escape if visited early

### 4. **Yves Saint Laurent (YSL) Museum**
- **Hours:** 10:00 AM–6:00 PM daily (closed Wednesdays); last entry 5:30 PM
- **Ticket:** 100 MAD (adult)
- **Advance booking:** **Mandatory online-only**; no window sales
- **Book at:** www.tickets.jardinmajorelle.com
- **Time needed:** 1–1.5 hours
- **Crowds:** Peak midday; quieter early opening (10 AM) or late afternoon
- **Combined tickets:** Available with Jardin Majorelle (recommended)
- **Best time:** 10–11 AM (opening) or after 4 PM
- **Notes:** Closed Wednesdays; book in advance from your home; adjacent to Majorelle Garden

### 5. **Le Jardin Secret**
- **Hours:** 9:30 AM–6:30 PM daily
- **Ticket:** 80 MAD (adult); 60 MAD (reduced); free for children under 12
- **Advance booking:** Optional online; walk-in available
- **Book at:** https://lejardinsecretmarrakech.com/boutique/
- **Time needed:** 1–1.5 hours
- **Crowds:** Peak 11 AM–3 PM; quieter early morning (9:30–10 AM) and after 5 PM
- **Best time:** Just after opening (9:30–10 AM) or after 5 PM
- **Notes:** Medina location; cooler than souks; good afternoon escape from heat

### 6. **Saadian Tombs**
- **Hours:** 9:00 AM–4:45 PM daily
- **Ticket:** 70 MAD (adult); free (children under 12)
- **Advance booking:** Not required; walk-in available
- **Payment:** Cash only (no credit cards)
- **Time needed:** 0.5–1 hour
- **Crowds:** Peak 11 AM–3 PM; quieter morning (9–10 AM) and late afternoon (after 4 PM)
- **Best time:** Early morning (9–9:30 AM) or after 4 PM
- **Notes:** Smaller site; fewer visitors than palaces; south medina location

### 7. **El Badi Palace**
- **Hours:** 9:00 AM–5:00 PM daily (last entry 4:30 PM)
- **Ticket:** 100 MAD (adult); 30 MAD (children under 12); 20 MAD (Moroccan residents)
- **Advance booking:** Optional online; walk-in available
- **Time needed:** 1.5–2 hours
- **Crowds:** Peak 11 AM–3 PM; quieter morning and late afternoon
- **Best time:** Early morning (9–10 AM) or after 4 PM
- **Notes:** Extensive ruins; less crowded than Bahia; allow time for upper levels and views

### 8. **Tanneries (Bab Debbagh)**
- **Hours:** Active throughout day; dyeing process peaks morning 9–11 AM
- **Ticket:** Free access to ground level; minimal tipping expected; guides optional
- **Advance booking:** Not required; walk-in access via surrounding shops
- **Tour booking:** Available via GetYourGuide; 30–60 MAD typical guide tip
- **Time needed:** 0.75–1.5 hours
- **Crowds:** Peak 11 AM–2 PM; quieter early morning (8:30–9:30 AM) and late afternoon (4–6 PM)
- **Best time:** Weekday morning (9–11 AM) for most active dyeing; Friday morning (before noon prayer)
- **Notes:** Located in eastern medina (Bab Debbagh district); sensory experience (smell); rooftop access via surrounding leather shops

### 9. **Maison de la Photographie**
- **Hours:** 9:30 AM–7:00 PM daily
- **Ticket:** 40–50 MAD (adult); free (children under 15/16); includes Music Museum access
- **Advance booking:** Not available online; payment at entrance (cash or card)
- **Time needed:** 1 hour
- **Crowds:** Relatively quiet throughout day
- **Best time:** Any time; good rainy-day alternative
- **Notes:** Small museum; less crowded than major sites; medina location adjacent to Le Foundouk restaurant

## Friday Considerations (Sep 20, 2026)

- **Museum/palace hours:** Unchanged; all run normal schedules
- **Medina shops/souks:** Midday (noon–3 PM) closures for Friday prayers; many small shops shut
- **Best souk time on Friday:** Before noon or after 3 PM
- **Tanneries:** Open all day; morning dyeing remains active despite prayer times
- **Strategy:** Visit museums/palaces on Friday morning (avoid midday) to leverage normal hours; save souks/tanneries for Friday late afternoon (3 PM+) when shops reopen

## Recommended Itinerary: Morning/Afternoon Split to Avoid Heat & Crowds

### **Optimal Strategy: East Medina Morning → Gardens Afternoon → Souks/Tanneries Late Afternoon**

#### **Morning (8:00 AM–12:30 PM)**
Start early in the **eastern medina** before heat peaks and crowds arrive:
1. **Bahia Palace** (8:00–9:30 AM) — 1.5 hours, entry at 9 AM opening
2. **Ben Youssef Madrasa** (9:45–11:00 AM) — 1.25 hours, adjacent
3. **Saadian Tombs** (11:15 AM–12:00 PM) — 0.75 hours, south medina
4. **Brief souk browse** (12:00–12:30 PM) — cooling off before lunch

**Heat status:** Manageable; move between sites quickly. Temps ~25–28°C (mid-morning in Sep).

#### **Midday Rest (12:30–3:30 PM)**
- **Lunch + siesta** at riad or café in shade; avoid peak heat and crowds
- Explore riad's courtyard if amenities allow
- Hydrate, rest in air conditioning
- Temps peak ~35°C (afternoon high in September)

#### **Late Afternoon (3:30–7:00 PM)**
Move to **cooler garden zones** and **re-energized souks:**
1. **Jardin Majorelle** (3:30–5:00 PM) — 1.5 hours; gardens coolest in late afternoon light
   - *If visiting YSL Museum:* 4:00–5:15 PM (100 MAD, advance booking required)
2. **Le Jardin Secret** (5:15–6:30 PM) — 1.25 hours; can skip if time-pressed
3. **Tanneries** (5:00–6:00 PM) — 1 hour; dyeing still active; rooftop views golden
4. **Souks** (6:00–7:30 PM) — Late light, most shops reopened post-prayer, lively but manageable

**Heat status:** Cooling toward evening; light ideal for photography. Temps drop to ~28–32°C.

## Key Tactics to Beat Heat & Crowds

1. **Pre-book YSL Museum** from home before arrival; book Jardin Majorelle online
2. **Start 8 AM sharp** at Bahia Palace; avoid 9 AM entrance bottleneck by arriving first
3. **Midday siesta non-negotiable** in Sep; 12:30–3:30 PM rest in riad avoids peak heat (35°C+)
4. **Gardens in late afternoon:** Majorelle is cooler 4–6 PM than morning; YSL feels less crowded 4–5 PM
5. **Tanneries weekday morning (9–11 AM) for dye activity**, Friday morning if you must; late afternoon trade-off is beauty of light
6. **Souks: Before 10 AM OR after 3 PM** to avoid midday crush; Friday after 3 PM for fewer shop closures
7. **Maison de la Photographie** as rain-day backup or final evening stop (open until 7 PM)

2. **Jardin Majorelle pricing aligns with YSL Museum** (both 100 MAD); combined tickets available and recommended.

## Recommendation: Execute Two-Session Model

**Do not attempt an all-day palace and souk marathon in September.** The heat (35°C+ midday) and crowds make it counterproductive. Instead:

1. **Morning session (8 AM–12:30 PM):** Eastern medina palaces (Bahia, Ben Youssef, Saadian Tombs) in rapid sequence while cool and shops just opening. Walk quickly; photograph less.
2. **Siesta (12:30–3:30 PM):** Mandatory rest at riad with air conditioning, hydration, light snacks. This is not laziness; it's survival and strategy.
3. **Late-afternoon session (3:30–7:30 PM):** Gardens (Majorelle, YSL, Le Jardin Secret optional) while cooling, then tanneries and souks as shops reopen and evening light improves.

**Pre-trip checklist:**
- Book YSL Museum online (mandatory; 100 MAD)
- Book Jardin Majorelle online (recommended; 100 MAD)
- Confirm Riad Sabah siesta accommodation (A/C, quiet hour 1–4 PM)
- Locate nearest ATM for Saadian Tombs (cash-only)

**Day-of timing (example: Friday Sep 20):**
- 8:00 AM: Bahia Palace
- 9:45 AM: Ben Youssef Madrasa
- 11:15 AM: Saadian Tombs
- 12:30 PM: Return to riad, lunch, rest
- 3:30 PM: Jardin Majorelle (advance booking covers entry)
- 4:15 PM: YSL Museum (if interested; book in advance)
- 5:15 PM: Le Jardin Secret (optional)
- 5:00–6:00 PM: Tanneries (late afternoon is acceptable compromise; dyeing less active but golden light)
- 6:00–7:30 PM: Souks (shops reopened post-3 PM prayer, lively but manageable)
