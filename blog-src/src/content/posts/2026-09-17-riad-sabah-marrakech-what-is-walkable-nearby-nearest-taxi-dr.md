## Summary

Riad Sabah sits in the Kasbah area of the medina (Derb Lamnabha), 4 km from Menara Airport. Within 15 minutes' walk: Saadian Tombs, El Badii Palace, Bahia Palace, Café Clock (best coffee), restaurants with tadelakt courtyards, and Jemaa el-Fnaa. Taxis drop at the nearest gate—likely Bab Agnaou or Bab er-Robb (5–10 min walk to riad). **Best approach:** Contact the riad beforehand (+212.524.38.90.34) to confirm exact drop-off gate and arrange a greeter. From airport on arrival: official taxi 150–160 MAD (~£18), ALSA bus L19 30 MAD (~£3), or ask riad to arrange pickup (typically €15). Departure Wed 23 Sep: leave by 8:30 AM for a 12 PM flight (30 min drive + 1.5–2 hrs processing).

## From Prior Context

The "Marrakech neighbourhoods" research (note_0c3b599a) documented the character of Kasbah and how to move between medina areas. That work confirmed: the Kasbah is quieter than Jemaa el-Fnaa, petit taxis run 20–30 MAD for medina trips, and the south medina (where Riad Sabah sits) is best explored on foot rather than by car.

## New Findings

### Location & Layout

**Exact Address:** Derb Lamnabha No. 13, Kasbah, Medina, Marrakech 40000. The riad is positioned between the king's palace (Palais Royal) and the Saadian Tombs—one of the most atmospheric corners of the medina.

**Distance from Airport:** 4–6 km, typically 15–20 minutes by car in normal traffic. Allow 25–30 minutes during rush hours or on arrival days when traffic peaks.

### What's Within a 15-Minute Walk

**Sights:**
- **Saadian Tombs** — 5-min walk south. Morocco's finest 16th-century royal mausoleum; entrance fee ~50 MAD (~£5).
- **El Badii Palace** — 10-min walk. Cavernous ruins; free but ask riad for current hours.
- **Bahia Palace** — 10-min walk. Lavish 19th-century mansion with gardens; ~100 MAD (~£10).
- **Koutoubia Mosque** — 1.7 km walk or one petit taxi hop (10 MAD). Marrakech's icon; can't enter if not Muslim, but the plaza and gardens are free and beautiful.
- **Jemaa el-Fnaa** — 15-min walk through the souks or via Rue Arset Aouzal. The medina's beating heart; best visited early morning (8–11 AM) to avoid crowds, or evening after 7 PM for food stalls and street performers.

**Cafés & Food:**
- **Café Clock** — Kasbah's best coffee spot. Open-air terrace upstairs; order avocado juice or their signature camel burger. Walk-in; no reservation needed. ~60 MAD (~£6) for coffee + pastry.
- **La Table de la Kasbah** — Traditional tanjia and tagines. Tables face Moulay Yazid Mosque; peaceful setting. ~150–200 MAD (~£15–20) per person.
- **Jemaa el-Fnaa Food Stalls** — Evening food vendors under umbrellas. Grilled meat, harira soup, fresh juice. 30–50 MAD (~£3–5) per dish.
- **Souk Restaurants** — Along the main souks between Riad Sabah and Jemaa el-Fnaa. Ask locals or riad staff for current favorites; fares 100–200 MAD.

**Pharmacy & ATM:**
- **Pharmacie Saadiens** — Rue Boutouil Kasbah (ask riad staff for exact direction; in the Kasbah, 5-min walk).
- **ATM locations** — Multiple around Jemaa el-Fnaa square (southwest corner, and along the avenue to the mosque). No ATMs directly in narrow medina streets; expect to walk 10–15 min.
- **Money exchange** — WAFA bureau de change (south side of Jemaa el-Fnaa, Rue Moulay Ismail; Mon–Fri 8 AM–7 PM, Sat 9 AM–1 PM & 2–6 PM).

### Taxi Drop-Off: Which Gate?

**Critical:** Taxis cannot drive into the medina's UNESCO-protected streets. The closest your taxi can reach is one of the medina's historic gates (babs). From Riad Sabah's location in the Kasbah, likely gates are:

- **Bab Agnaou** — South gate, 5–10 min walk to riad. Best for arrivals from Jemaa el-Fnaa side.
- **Bab er-Robb** — East/Kasbah gate, 5–8 min walk. Less crowded.
- **Bab Taghzout** — West gate, ~10 min walk but easier to find if coming from Gueliz side.

**How to Find the Riad from the Drop-Off:**

1. **Before arrival:** Contact Riad Sabah and ask which gate is closest and request a staff member to meet you there. Phone: +212.524.38.90.34 or +212.667.44.50.44. Email: contact@riadsabah.com.
2. **At the gate:** Look for someone holding a sign with your name (if arranged), or give them a call. Most riads have staff standing by for this.
3. **Walking through narrow streets:** The paths are maze-like but well-trodden. Stay alert for scooters and water carts. Luggage on wheels is awkward; a shoulder bag is better.
4. **GPS/maps:** Google Maps does not work reliably in medina alleyways. Don't rely on it; follow the person guiding you or ask locals "Derb Lamnabha?" as you walk.

### Airport Transfers (Both Ways)

#### **Arrival (Thu 17 Sep)**

**Option 1: Official Petit Taxi**
- Cost: 150–160 MAD (~£18) fixed fare from taxi counter at airport arrivals.
- How: Exit Terminal 2, go to taxi ticket counter, pay, then board assigned vehicle.
- Time: 20–30 min to medina.
- Drop-off: Medina gate (see above). Driver may not know "Riad Sabah" by name; best to have riad's name written in Arabic or pre-arrange.
- **Best for:** Flexible arrival time, budget travelers.

**Option 2: ALSA Bus Line 19**
- Cost: 30 MAD (~£3) one-way, 50 MAD (~£6) round-trip.
- How: Exit Terminal 2 arrivals, look for bus stop just past taxi area. Departs every 20 minutes 6 AM–11:30 PM.
- Time: 25–40 min, depending on stops (serves Jemaa el-Fnaa, Gueliz, train station, bus station).
- Drop-off: Jemaa el-Fnaa or Gueliz station. From there, take a petit taxi (10–20 MAD) to medina gate, or walk 15 min.
- **Best for:** Budget-conscious, not time-sensitive.

**Option 3: Riad-Arranged Pickup**
- Cost: Typically €15 (around 160 MAD / £15) if arranged in advance, sometimes free for stays over 4 nights.
- How: Email/call riad when booking and request airport pickup. They'll arrange a car and meeting point.
- Time: Direct to riad, 20–30 min. Staff may meet you at gate or walk you through medina.
- **Best for:** Stress-free arrival, especially with luggage. **Recommended.**

**Option 4: Private Pre-Booked Transfer**
- Cost: €15 per car (same as riad arrangement, but through external services like GetYourGuide or Viator).
- How: Book online 24–48 hours ahead.
- Time: Direct, 20–30 min.

#### **Departure (Wed 23 Sep)**

**Timing:**
- Flight departure time: Assume ~12 PM (noon). Arrive at airport 2.5–3 hours early (by 9:00–9:30 AM latest).
- Drive time airport: 20–30 min from medina.
- **Leave riad by 8:00–8:30 AM** to be safe.

**How to Arrange:**
- **Best option:** Ask riad to arrange a car the night before. Cost: 150–180 MAD (~£18–22).
- **Petit taxi:** Hail from medina street or ask riad staff. Agree fare beforehand (typically 150–160 MAD). Reliable but driver may get lost finding riad entrance; have riad meet you outside.
- **ALSA bus:** Does not go to airport from medina (only airport → medina).

**Luggage Tips:**
- Petit taxis are small (max 3 passengers + bags). For 2 adults + 2 large suitcases, luggage may be tight; negotiate before getting in.
- If staying a final night with luggage, ask riad if you can leave bags in a storeroom until departure time.
- Airport baggage drop-off: All luggage must be checked in at the airline desk (2+ hours before flight). No lounge access unless you have premium status.

**Processing Time at Airport:**
- Pre-check-in screening: 10–15 min.
- Check-in & boarding pass: 30–45 min (busy times can be 1+ hour).
- Security & passport control: 20–45 min depending on crowd.
- **Total: 1.5–2.5 hours** once you enter the terminal. Arriving 2.5–3 hours early covers this comfortably.

## Recommendation

**For arrival (Thu 17 Sep):** **Riad-arranged pickup is worth the cost.** Call or email Riad Sabah the day before you book and ask for airport pickup. Cost is roughly the same as an official taxi (€15–20), but you avoid the gate confusion and get direct access to the riad with staff to help navigate the narrow streets and unload luggage. They know the medina routes.

**For departure (Wed 23 Sep):** **Ask your riad to arrange the car the night before.** Provide your flight time and departure time (aim for 8:00–8:30 AM). Same cost (~150–180 MAD / £18–22), same reliability, and no hunting for a taxi at dawn.

**Walkable Exploration:** Don't miss Saadian Tombs and El Badii Palace—they're the soul of this part of the medina. Jemaa el-Fnaa is 15 minutes away but plan to visit early (8–11 AM) or late (after 7 PM) to avoid afternoon crowds and heat. Café Clock is genuinely the best coffee spot in the Kasbah; sit on the terrace and watch the streets.

**Key Contacts:**
- Riad Sabah: +212.524.38.90.34 / +212.667.44.50.44 / contact@riadsabah.com
- Arrival: Mention you have luggage and ask them to have someone meet you at the gate
- Departure: Confirm pickup time the morning you're leaving

**Currency:** All prices in MAD (Moroccan Dirham). Rough conversion: 10 MAD ≈ £1. ATMs in Jemaa el-Fnaa area are reliable; withdraw cash early in your stay.
