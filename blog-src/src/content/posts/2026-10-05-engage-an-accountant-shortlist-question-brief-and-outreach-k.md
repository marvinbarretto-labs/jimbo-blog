## Question brief (send as one document)

**Company:** Fourfold, single-director UK Ltd. Year end 30 September 2026, now closed. No accountant is currently engaged. Marvin has not decided any treatment below and is seeking advice on each point.

**1. First question: client income received into a personal account.**
- The Wise USD account is personal. A business account application was attempted and was problematic.
- BME OPERATING, a client, paid **$39,300 across January to May 2026** into that personal account.
- Fourfold's revenue for the year to 30 September 2026 is currently recorded as roughly £30,500, on the assumption that this was company income.
- Nothing is currently reserved for Corporation Tax.
- Questions: How should this income have been treated, and how should it be treated now? What is Fourfold's revenue and Corporation Tax position for the year to 30 September 2026? Does anything need correcting for prior periods?

**2. Extraction versus the borrowing spread.** Marvin can borrow at about 3.66% while £73,708 of company cash earns 0.85%. The cash belongs to the company. Question: what are the options and consequences of extracting funds, and how should the spread be weighed?

**3. Company investing reserves.** Total company assets are about £96k. Question: what are the consequences of the company investing its reserves, for the company's status and for reliefs on any eventual winding up, and how are investment returns taxed relative to trading profit?

**4. Company buying a van.** Questions: benefit-in-kind on any personal use, capital allowances, and whether renting the van out would count as trading activity the company currently lacks.

**5. Corporation Tax for the year ended 30 September 2026.** A liability may exist and nothing is reserved. Question: what should be calculated and set aside, and by when?

**6. Filing deliberately from next year.** Salary has drifted to £0 and accountancy fees have drifted to nothing, neither by decision. About £2,131 of business tooling sits on personal cards, so the company cannot see it. Questions: what should the company's pay, expense and fee arrangements be, and how should the tooling spend be recorded?

**7. Scope and fees.** Is this a compliance engagement, an advice engagement, or both? What is the fixed fee for each, including a one-off advisory session?

## Call checklist

- [ ] Send the one-page brief 24 hours before the call
- [ ] Confirm the firm is regulated (ICAEW, ACCA or equivalent) and carries professional indemnity insurance
- [ ] Ask whether they take non-trading companies, and what a typical engagement looks like
- [ ] Put question 1 (BME income) first, then work down the brief
- [ ] Ask for fixed fees in writing, for compliance and for advice, with scope and exclusions
- [ ] Ask whether prior-period correction work is billed separately
- [ ] Ask who the named accountant is, and their response time
- [ ] Ask about notice period and any minimum term
- [ ] Ask what they need from Marvin and by when
- [ ] Record each answer in the debrief template below before the end of the day
