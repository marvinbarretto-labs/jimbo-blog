## Summary
Renew **online** at passports.govt.nz (application portal: https://dia.services.govt.nz/passports-getting-started/). Paper by post exists but is slower. You need a RealMe login, a digital photo meeting spec, an identity referee, and a payment card. The referee is the long pole, so the next action is to ask one now.

## New findings

**Method: online vs post** — Online is the fastest option. Paper applications "take longer than online as they need extra processing" [1]. Renewal online needs age 16+, an existing passport (expired is fine), and a RealMe login [2]. Paper forms (PAS300) can be posted to the NZ office in London, which does not take in-person submissions [3]. The paper claim comes from a third-party site, so I haven't confirmed it with an official source.

**Required for online renewal** [1][4]
- RealMe login
- Your current or expired NZ passport
- Digital passport photo (spec below)
- Identity referee (criteria below)
- Credit, debit or Prezzy card
- Proof of name change, if applicable [3]

**Photo spec** [5]
- Colour portrait, 3:4 aspect ratio
- .jpg/.jpeg, 250 KB–5 MB
- 900–4500 px wide, 1200–6000 px high
- Under 6 months old
- Scans of printed photos are not accepted. Ask the photographer to email you the digital file.

**Identity referee** [5]
- Aged 16+
- Has known you more than 1 year
- Holds a current or expired NZ passport
- Agrees to be your referee and is contactable by phone or email
- Not family (including cousins, in-laws, ex-spouses), not your partner, and not living at your address

**Cost**
- NZ domestic adult renewal is NZ$247 [1].
- A third-party site lists the UK fee as £147 standard, £294 urgent (3 working days), plus about £14 courier [3].
- I could not load the official fees page (HTTP 403), so treat the GBP figures as unverified. The portal checkout will show the exact amount before you pay.

**Processing time**
- Official domestic guidance is up to 20 working days. Urgent is aimed at 3 working days [1][2].
- A third-party UK guide says standard is up to 10 working days plus courier delivery [3]. A general guide says allow 6–8 weeks minimum when overseas [2].
- The sources disagree. Budget 6–8 weeks, plus however long it takes to find a referee.

## Recommendation
Do it online. The referee, not the form, is what has stalled this for 86 days, so line that up first. Check your passport's expiry date at the same time. Many countries require 3–6 months of validity remaining on entry, so if you have travel booked, pay for urgent.

**Next action (under 30 minutes):** Message one NZ-passport-holding friend who is not family and does not live with you, and who has known you over a year. Ask: "Can I name you as my identity referee for my NZ passport renewal? You'll get an email or call to confirm you know me, and you'll need your NZ passport (expired is fine)." Then book a photo with a photographer who can email you the digital file.
