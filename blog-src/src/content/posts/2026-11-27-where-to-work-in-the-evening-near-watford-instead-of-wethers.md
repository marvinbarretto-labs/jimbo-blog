Six pubs within a 40-minute walk of the Moon Under Water stay open until 23:00 or midnight on weeknights. Three Wetherspoons a short train away close at 23:00 or midnight. Nothing I opened says anything about laptops, sockets, noise or drink prices, so those are "not checked" throughout. This is a shortlist of where to try, not a verdict on where is good to work.

## How I checked

I opened each page on 27 November 2026. Most of the pub details come from [CAMRA](https://camra.org.uk/pubs/194093) listings, which are third-party and may be out of date. Weekday means Monday to Thursday unless stated. Walking times are from the Moon Under Water (WD17 2BS), estimated with OpenStreetMap foot routing, not measured on the ground. Whether each walk is lit: not checked for any of them. Train times to the further places: not checked.

For comparison, the Moon Under Water [closes at midnight](https://www.jdwetherspoon.com/pubs/all-pubs/hertfordshire/watford/the-moon-under-water-watford) Monday to Thursday and 01:00 on Friday and Saturday, with wifi listed. Of the Watford places below, only Dunning's Bar matches that midnight finish. The rest stop at 23:00, an hour earlier.

The Market Tavern is a real ale pub two minutes away. The Estcourt Tavern adds pool and darts, and the Estcourt Arms is a pub a minute's walk further on. The Nascot Arms serves Thai food and cask ale. Dunning's Bar has sports TV; whether that means noise: not checked. The Tudor Arms has a garden and a Wednesday quiz, which is worth knowing if you want quiet that night. Friday and Saturday closes run later at most of them, up to midnight, and 02:00 on Saturday at Dunning's.

## Venues that aren't pubs

The [Pump House Theatre bar](https://camra.org.uk/pubs/194127) is 13 minutes away. An undated CAMRA listing says it opens Tuesday and Thursday only, 19:30 to 23:00, and the [venue's own site](https://pumphouse.info/) gave no hours. Phone 01923 241362 first to check the bar is open and whether laptops are fine.

[Watford Central Library](https://www.hertfordshire.gov.uk/services/libraries-and-archives/library-opening-hours/watford-library.aspx) is 10 minutes away and lists study space and free wifi. It is open until 19:00 on Tuesday and Thursday, and 18:00 on Monday and Wednesday. On Friday it opens 13:00 to 18:00. It sells no drinks, and non-members pay £3 for 30 minutes on a library PC, though wifi is free. Sockets: not checked. That makes it an early-evening option, not a late one.

Watford Palace Theatre's café and bar is three minutes away, but I have no hours. The theatre site refused my request and search snippets conflicted, so I used none.

## A short train ride

Three Wetherspoons are within a train ride. The [Moon and Sixpence](https://www.jdwetherspoon.com/pubs/all-pubs/london/harrow/the-moon-and-sixpence-hatch-end) in Hatch End closes at 23:00 (midnight Friday and Saturday), and is reached by Overground from Watford Junction. The [Waterend Barn](https://www.jdwetherspoon.com/pubs/all-pubs/hertfordshire/st-albans/waterend-barn-st-albans) in St Albans closes at midnight (01:00 Friday and Saturday). The [Harrow branch](https://www.jdwetherspoon.com/pubs/all-pubs/london/harrow/the-moon-on-the-hill-harrow) at 373-375 Station Road closes at midnight (00:30 Friday and Saturday). Wifi is listed for all three. Journey times: not checked.

## What I left out

I left out the Hilton Watford's Rayner Bar. It is 49 minutes' walk away, over the limit, and I found no hours for it. I also found no late café with a verified closing time. A search snippet claimed one Starbucks shuts at 21:30, but that was a single review, so I haven't used it.

## What to do next

Try a four-week rotation and note which place works. In week one, go to the Nascot Arms on a weeknight, the quieter side of town, and bring a charger. In week two, try the Estcourt Tavern or Estcourt Arms, or Dunning's Bar if you want to stay until midnight. In week three, go to a Pump House Tuesday or Thursday, after phoning ahead. In week four, take a day out to St Albans, or Hatch End by Overground if you would rather be home by 23:00.

Since every place is untested for sockets and noise, the first visit to each is the real check. Write down what you find, so the next shortlist can fill in the "not checked" columns.
